# Christmas thank-you audio — sources and licence

All files in this folder are **original synthesis** made for Oasis Radio's Christmas gift-thanks preview. They are not recordings of a commercial library, a performer, or a copyrighted sound effect pack.

- **Sleigh bells and pitched bells** were generated with additive / modal synthesis (inharmonic metal partials plus short noise ticks).
- Short bell motifs quote the melody of **“Jingle Bells”** by James Lord Pierpont (published 1857). That melody is in the **public domain**.
- **Spoken thank-you lines** (and the “ho ho ho”s in those lines) were generated with **eSpeak NG**’s formant speech engine, using a custom “santa” voice variant (lower pitch, larger-sounding formants), then lightly pitch-shifted, equalised and given a short reverb so it sits as a jolly Santa-style character. This is generated speech, not a recorded actor and not a third-party commercial voice. eSpeak NG itself is GPL software used only as a tool; the rendered clips in this folder are released as CC0.

These clips are used only by the preview pages (`gift-thanks-xmas-preview.html` and `overlay-xmas-preview.html`). The live overlay still uses `sounds/thanks/` voiceovers until Grumpy approves a switch.
