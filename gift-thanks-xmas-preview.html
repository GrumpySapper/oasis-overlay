What you’ll hear

Each gift plays a sleigh-bell jingle, then a Santa ho ho ho. Bigger gifts get a longer public-domain “Jingle Bells” motif and more hos (Heart Me: one soft ho; small: one; medium/request: two; big: three; huge: four; legend: five). Bursts are throttled: one thank-you per gifter if they combo, a short gap between clips, and a cap of six waiting so a rush of roses doesn’t become a pile-up. Peaks sit around −3 dBFS so they shouldn’t shout over the music; there is a volume slider here and ?vol=0.1–1 / ?sound=0 on the overlay preview.

Audio sources

All clips are original synthesis, CC0 1.0. The bell motifs quote the public-domain melody of “Jingle Bells” (James Lord Pierpont, 1857). The hos are a generated Santa voice, not a recorded actor. Details in SOURCES.md.
