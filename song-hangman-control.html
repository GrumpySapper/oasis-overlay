How viewers play (live in TikTok chat)
Press Next puzzle, then tell chat: "Type ONE letter to guess, or type the song title to solve it!"
• A single letter (A–Z) is a guess. Right letters light up; each wrong letter adds a piece to the snowman (7 lives).
• Typing the song title solves it (small typos are fine, the band isn't needed). Ordinary chat is ignored.
• Each viewer can guess every 4 seconds; letters already guessed are free and ignored.
When a round ends, press Next puzzle for another, or Clear the board to go back to "Waiting…". Studio layer: song-hangman.html (add ?bg=clear for a transparent background). The answer only shows on this page once your key is saved.
